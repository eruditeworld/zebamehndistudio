About Zeba Mehndi Studio

Preserving Heritage, Crafting Contemporary Elegance

Located on Jagat Narayan Road near City Station Bridge in Aminabad, Zeba Mehndi Studio is a sanctuary dedicated to the timeless art of Indian mehndi. We view every design not merely as decoration, but as an intimate reflection of tradition, joy, and individual beauty.

From opulent royal bridal patterns to light, airy modern Arabic motifs, our work is defined by delicate fine-line work, deep respect for cultural symbolism, and the comfort of our clients during their most special celebrations.

100% Natural Henna

Freshly blended with pure essential oils for skin safety and a deep, rich mahogany stain.

Meticulous Precision

Clean symmetry, delicate grid jaals, and intricate storytelling elements tailored to you.

Studio & Doorstep Service

Appointments hosted at our comfortable studio or arranged directly at your wedding venue.

Personalized Consultation

Collaborate on motifs that harmoniously complement your wedding lehenga and theme.
