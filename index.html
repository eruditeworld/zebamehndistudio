About Zeba Mehndi Studio

At Zeba Mehndi Studio, mehndi is more than a tradition — it is an expression of joy, beauty and personal celebration. Every design begins with a conversation, so the patterns on your hands feel entirely your own.

From delicate bridal motifs to bold, contemporary Arabic lines, each application is approached with patience, precision and genuine care — because your celebration deserves nothing less.

"Every hand tells a different story. My work is simply to help tell it beautifully."
